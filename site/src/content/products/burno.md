## Introduction

Burno is a full-body workout played with a deck of cards. You don't need real cards: the app shuffles and deals for you, keeps the time and counts every rep. **Flip. Burn. Repeat.**

I built it because my schedule kept beating my gym plans. Some days there just isn't time to get to a gym, but there's always a bit of floor, and usually a pull-up bar somewhere nearby. That's all Burno needs.

## How it works

1. **Pick your moves.** Each suit gets an exercise. Keep the classics, swap in your own, or tap *Shuffle moves* for a random set: no gear, gear, or a mix.
2. **Flip a card.** The suit is the move and the number is the reps. Aces are a 20-rep wildcard.
3. **Clear the deck.** Keep flipping until it's gone, with no skipping. Face cards earn a breather, and a joker asks you to pick between a 60-second rest and 30 seconds all out.

A half deck is a quick 15-minute hit; a full deck is the whole 45-minute session. You can do it anywhere (park, living room, hotel room) with just your body weight or a pair of dumbbells, and with friends everyone does the same card or takes turns dealing.

## After the workout

- A summary of your pace, per card and per rep, with a chart showing where you sped up or slowed down
- A share card that saves your workout as an image
- A count of every Burno workout done so far, plus everyone's reps added up (only card and rep counts are sent, nothing personal)
- Ratings and feedback, straight from the summary screen

Burno is free with no ads, supported by Ko-fi donations. It launched on 6 October 2026.

**Stack:** Vite + React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, GitHub Pages, Google Apps Script.
