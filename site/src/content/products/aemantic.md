Aemantic is a multi-agent AI finance assistant. It reads filings from SEC EDGAR and holdings from IBKR, and works over them with agents built on LangChain and MCP servers.

It's still in development: I'm building and testing it in a dev environment, currently on the **Phoenix** iteration.

**Stack:** FastAPI, LangChain, MCP servers, SEC EDGAR, IBKR, AWS (multi-account).
