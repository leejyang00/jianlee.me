---
title: "Aemantic: the X-ray dashboard is the new home page"
date: 2026-10-01
product: aemantic
---

The chat moved to `/chat`, and the home page is now a dashboard of your saved investments. Its first panel, "What's really inside", looks through your funds to the companies they hold and shows how much of every $100 you have sits in each one, with a link to the SEC report each number comes from.

Smaller things from the same week: you pick tickers from a search list instead of typing them from memory, share-class tickers like BRK.B now work, and funds we can't look inside (SPY, GLD, IBIT) say so instead of showing up as companies. A third panel, "New since you last looked", lists SEC filings from your companies over the last 30 days.
