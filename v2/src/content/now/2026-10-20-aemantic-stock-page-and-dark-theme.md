---
title: "Aemantic: a page for any stock, and a denser dark theme"
date: 2026-10-20
product: aemantic
---

There's now a page for any US stock or fund, no portfolio needed. It has an overview, the company's financials by year and its recent SEC filings, and it deliberately shows no price and no "add to my investments" button. The goal is to explain a company, not rank it.

The dashboard got a rework too. It now has a dense dark theme as the default, the first panel takes the full row with the next two side by side, and the glossary terms feed one help bar instead of repeating in every card. Long lists can be closed again with "Show fewer". On the backend, comparing funds now counts foreign stocks that were missing before and no longer treats a money-market fund as a company. This is all in dev.
