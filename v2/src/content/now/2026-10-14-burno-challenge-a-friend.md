You can now challenge a friend to your workout. Send a link and they play the exact same deck in the same order. When they finish, the summary shows who won and offers a rematch. The challenge sheet asks for your name first, so it doesn't hide behind the buttons on small phones.

Smaller changes: the reps panel now shows which suits call which move, and highlights the suit of the card in play, so you don't have to flip a card to remember the plan. Before the first flip there's a Back button instead of End, so opening a workout by mistake no longer counts as a finished one. The share sheet also has a Transparent option: just the stats, to lay over your own photo.
