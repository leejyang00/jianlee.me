---
title: "Aemantic: stock pages get Risks, five years of figures and the company's own words"
date: 2026-10-31
product: aemantic
---

The single-stock page was reworked. Overview and Financials sit side by side, Filings has its own row, and there's a new Risks panel that quotes the headlines from the company's latest annual report. Financials now cover five fiscal years, with new tiles for revenue, gross margin and R&D, and colour that only shows direction, not good or bad.

Overview now opens with the company's own description of itself: the first sentence or two of the business section of its 10-K, quoted word for word and linked to the filing. I don't write my own description of a company. Labels also follow the SEC's own names now, so it says "Revenue" rather than "Sales".

Banks were the awkward case. Morgan Stanley showed no revenue and some risk sections wouldn't parse, because banks tag and title things differently. Net revenues are now read as revenue, bank tables size by net income, and the 10-K reader handles more heading styles.
