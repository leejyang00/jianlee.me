Face cards and jokers have picture art now, number cards show the right number of pips, and jokers come with a countdown and a hard move to pick. Rep totals are hidden during a workout by default, so you focus on the card in front of you.

The summary shows your pace, and a community counter on the home and summary screens adds up everyone's workouts and reps. Only card and rep counts are sent, nothing personal. There's also a "What's new" button with a dated changelog.
