---
title: "Aemantic: quarters, what changed in the risks, and a More numbers switch"
date: 2026-11-14
product: aemantic
---

Stock pages got three additions. Financials has a Years | Quarters switch that shows the last five quarters, with each quarter's revenue compared against the same quarter a year earlier so seasons don't distort it. Companies don't file a Q4 report, so I work it out as the 10-K's full year minus the first three quarters and mark it with a footnote saying so.

The Risks panel can now show what changed since last year's 10-K. New headings and reworded ones are flagged, reworded ones show last year's wording underneath with the changed words marked on both sides, and risks that were dropped are listed separately. The panel says plainly that a change is what the company chose to change, not a sign of what will happen.

A Simple | More numbers switch sits by the company name. Simple stays the default. More numbers adds EPS, cash flow, shares and dividends, and cash against debt, with the quarters view behind it. Where a figure was adjusted for a stock split, or a debt total was added up from parts, a footnote says how and shows the as-filed number. If a bank has no capital-spending line, the page says why there's no free cash flow.
