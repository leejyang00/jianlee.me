---
title: "Aemantic: quarterly figures, what changed in the risks, and a More numbers view"
date: 2026-11-14
product: aemantic
---

Stock pages got three additions, in dev for now. Financials can switch between years and the last five quarters, each with revenue, the change on the same quarter a year before, gross margin, net income and net margin. A fourth quarter has no quarterly report of its own, so it's worked out as the 10-K's full year minus the first three quarters, and marked with a footnote that says so. Each quarter links to the filings it came from.

The Risks panel can now show only what changed since last year's 10-K: headings that are new this year, ones that were reworded, with last year's wording underneath and the changed words marked on both sides, and ones that were dropped. A note under it says that a new or reworded risk is what the company chose to change, not a sign of what will happen.

There's also a Simple | More numbers switch. Simple keeps four tiles and the yearly figures. More numbers adds diluted EPS, P/E, free cash flow, cash against debt, shares and dividend per share, plus the quarterly view. Figures adjusted for a stock split are marked and the footnote gives the numbers as filed, and the page remembers which view you picked.
