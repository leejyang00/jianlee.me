title: "Aemantic: a denser X-ray table and colour that only shows direction"
date: 2026-10-13
product: aemantic
---

The first panel of the dashboard, what's really inside your investments, is now one sortable table in a terminal style. Each company is a row, with columns for its share of your total, what you hold directly, and its weight in each fund you own. It fits all 25 rows on one screen instead of about six, and I dropped the "$X of every $100" wording.

I also settled a rule for colour: green and red may show direction only, always next to the + or − sign, and never as a verdict on a holding. The design system page now has dedicated up and down colours and spells out the rule. This is all in dev.
