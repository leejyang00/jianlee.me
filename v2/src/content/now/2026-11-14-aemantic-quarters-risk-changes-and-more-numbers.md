---
title: "Aemantic: quarters, what changed in the risks, and a More numbers view"
date: 2026-11-14
product: aemantic
---

The stock page got three additions. Financials has a Years | Quarters switch, and the quarters view shows the last five quarters with revenue compared against the same quarter a year earlier, so seasons don't distort the change. Quarters come from each 10-Q. There's no 10-Q for the fourth quarter, so I work it out as the full year from the 10-K minus the first nine months, and mark it with a footnote so it isn't mistaken for a filed figure.

Risks has a new All | What changed switch. It compares this year's risk headings with last year's 10-K and flags each one as new or reworded. A reworded risk shows last year's line underneath, with the dropped and added words marked.

There's also a Simple | More numbers switch. Simple stays the default. More numbers adds diluted EPS, P/E, free cash flow, share count and cash against debt, in a yearly table grouped into Profit and Cash. Free cash flow is operating cash flow minus capital spending, and every figure still cites the filing it came from.
