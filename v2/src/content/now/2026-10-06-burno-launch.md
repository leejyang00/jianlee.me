Launch day. What started as a 52-card workout page became Burno: a rebuilt app with a virtual deck, a timer and custom moves, a new name, a Sunrise palette and a flip-stack logo, served from its own domain.

The same day it got Shuffle moves (no gear, gear or a mix), a shareable workout summary card that saves straight to Photos on iPhone, star ratings and feedback after every workout, and a Ko-fi tip link.
