title: "Aemantic: what a company does, and where its revenue comes from"
date: 2026-11-19
product: aemantic

The Overview on stock pages now answers what the company does, in dev for now. Under the opening quote from the 10-K's business section, it adds two or three more of the company's own sentences from the same section, picked by AI and quoted with no words changed. The caption says they were chosen by AI. Code checks that every pick is a real sentence from the filing, and drops the whole answer if it isn't.

Below that, Where the money came from splits the latest year's revenue by product, by segment and by region, each only when the company files it. Every row has the company's own label, its share on a fixed 0 to 100% bar, so it doesn't read as a ranking, and the change on the year before. A cited line links the 10-K and says the shares and changes are worked out by Aemantic.
