The pace report now spells out your quickest and longest cards as two tiles, each with the time, the exercise and reps the card asked for, and the card named in full, like "Ace of Spades". Pace times use units throughout (8s, 1m 30s) instead of switching to clock style past a minute.

I also added analytics so I can see visits, where they come from and whether people return. It stores an anonymous id in local storage with no cookies, doesn't record sessions, and never sends custom move names or feedback text.
