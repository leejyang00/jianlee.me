---
title: "Aemantic: What's happened quotes the filing, plus a price, P/E and a market clock"
date: 2026-11-19
product: aemantic
---

Stock pages got a round of changes. The Filings panel became What's happened and looks back 90 days instead of 30. Each 8-K row gets a plain label from its Item number, such as Leadership, Results or Agreement, and quotes the filing's first sentence under that Item in its own words, with any cuts marked and no AI-written summary. A results filing adds that quarter's revenue, change on a year before and net income, but only once that quarter is in the page's figures, so it never shows the previous quarter's numbers. The list is now newest first.

The page shows a 15-minute delayed price, and More numbers works out P/E, market value and dividend yield from it, each written as the sum it is, with the filing it divides by. P/E uses diluted EPS over the last four quarters, and the EPS tile beside it now shows the same figure. P/E at the company's last five year ends sits underneath as plain text, so the company is only compared with its own past. Under the price there's a live market clock: New York time, whether the market is open, and your own time if you're elsewhere.

Two tidy-ups from early feedback: the XBRL tag names under Financials now sit behind a Sources toggle, and the Overview's SEC filing facts moved to a small cited line at the foot of the panel.
